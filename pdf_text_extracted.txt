We are Himaaya Organization, an institution committed to promoting 
social peace, human rights, and achieving sustainable development in local 
communities in Yemen. We believe in the importance of building a society 
based on justice, equality, and respect for human rights for all its members. 
Through our projects and programs, we strive to address the social, eco-
nomic, and political challenges facing local communities, working towards 
positive change, stability, and development. We provide the necessary sup-
port to local communities to positively influence political, social, and eco-
nomic processes. By relying on community partnerships and collaboration 
with local and international entities, we work to enhance individual and 
community capacities and provide the necessary support for sustainable 
development. Our mission is to achieve positive change and promote a cul-
ture of peace and justice in the communities we serve.

Peace, Protection and Law Foundation (SPPLPF) possesses flexible operational 
capacity and a well-established strategic presence in Yemen, with an increasing 
operational focus on areas under the control of the internationally recognized 
government. In response to changing conditions on the ground, we have adapted our 
operational structure to ensure the safety of our staff, maintain the independence of 
our operations, and enhance the effectiveness of our services to the communities 
most in need. Our presence is supported by official work permits, ensuring broad 
geographical coverage.
Current Status:
Accordingly, we now maintain a limited presence in Sana'a, restricted to a 
core team dedicated to implementing specific programs that require neutral 
engagement and specialized access, such as second-track legal support and 
mediation initiatives.
Legal & Geographical Scope:
This office holds a national-level work permit, providing the legal framework 
to operate across all governorates and districts under the control of de facto 
authorities, when required, within the scope of our specialized mandates.
Office Size:
A fully integrated main office serving as the command center for SPPLPF operations. 
The office currently houses our key operational and logistical teams, making it the 
primary hub for the planning, implementation, and management of high-budget 
programs in Yemen.
Legal & Geographical Scope:
Based on an official main permit, our operations cover all districts of Taiz Governorate. 
Through this core office, we manage and coordinate activities in neighboring 
governorates, including Lahj, providing us with direct access and enhanced response 
capacity in the southern regions.
Office Size:
A field branch office with strong operational and executive capabilities, 
operating under the direct supervision of the main office in Taiz.
Legal & Geographical Scope:
Our operations are supported by a branch permit covering all districts of 
Marib Governorate, enabling us to effectively respond to the critical needs of 
displaced and host communities in the region.
Office Capacity and Functions:
In line with humanitarian principles and best 
risk-management practices, and following the recent wave 
of arrests and crackdowns targeting humanitarian and 
development organizations in Sana'a, as well as operational 
complications resulting    from the FTO designation, the 
organization made a strategic decision to relocate its key         
         operational and logistical teams to our office in Taiz.
Operational Presence
Below is a full list of our offices and geographical coverage:
Taiz Governorate (Head Office & Core Operational Hub)
Resonate! Yemen (Lower Section)
Thanks to our operational structure and official permits, we have the capacity 
to operate in the following governorates:
- Taiz Governorate: We have the capacity to operate in all districts of the 
governorate from our main headquarters.
- Marib Governorate: We can cover all districts of the governorate through our 
branch office.
- Governorates Neighboring Taiz: From our operational hub in Taiz, we can 
expand our operations to include Lahj Governorate.
- Northern Governorates: Our national permit registered in Sana'a provides 
the legal framework to operate, within the scope of our specialized legal 
support and mediation programs, in all governorates under the control of de 
facto authorities.
Governorates Where We Can Operate:

Sana'a Governorate (Specialized Office for Specific Tasks)

Meeting with EU and Netherlands Ambassadors in Marib Governorate
The Marib branch team of the Foundation for the Protection of Law and Strengthening Social 
Peace held a meeting with the Ambassador of the European Union and the Ambassador of the 
Kingdom of the Netherlands, accompanied by their respective delegations.
The meeting provided an opportunity to discuss the Foundation’s work and ongoing activities 
in Marib Governorate, including the project currently being implemented with funding from 
the European Union Delegation to Yemen. The discussion also highlighted the Foundation’s 
operational presence and efforts to support communities in the governorate.

Participated in by (12) officers, the workshop aimed to enhance the participants' capacities in
